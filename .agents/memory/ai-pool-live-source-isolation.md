---
name: AI pool live-source isolation
description: Rules for feeding real-time evidence into the AI industry discovery pool without expanding protected radar authority.
---

AI pool targeted enrichment may consume only a bounded, independently verified focused-scan status for a non-protected symbol. A one-at-a-time rotating leader probe produces those candidates from the taxonomy; protected-symbol radar statuses must never be used as the pool's live qualification source.

**Why:** Reusing the protected five-symbol service means the broader pool never receives a genuine external candidate path and risks coupling its experimental lifecycle to production ranking, Alert, and Buy authority.

**How to apply:** Candidate admission requires the focused sidecar's own authorized leader, fresh live feed, reference, and confirmation gates. Keep leader probes bounded and rotate/cool down after routing so status events cannot duplicate an admission. Catch every asynchronous pool persistence or provider failure at the sidecar boundary; mark the pool unavailable/withheld in memory and never let the error become an unhandled process rejection.
