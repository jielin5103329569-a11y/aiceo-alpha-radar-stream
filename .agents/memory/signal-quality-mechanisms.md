---
name: Signal quality mechanisms
description: Durable rules for separating opportunity strength from data confidence and qualifying high-grade Alpha Radar stages.
---

Signal Score represents opportunity strength only. Data Confidence is separate and assesses data completeness, freshness, cross-context stability, and evidence consistency; a high opportunity score never substitutes for high confidence.

Multi-timeframe context must retain observed short, medium, and higher-window views of the same existing core features. A short-window move cannot independently justify a high-grade upgrade when the broader observed structure is unavailable, mixed, or contradictory.

Every high-grade stage must inspect available opposing evidence, including sector/market weakness where an independent source exists, selling pressure, price/volume divergence, breakout failure, and structural support loss. Missing independent evidence must remain explicitly unavailable rather than inferred. Strong observed opposition reduces or prevents promotion.

All three mechanisms serve the independent pre-breakout, true-breakout, and post-breakout/trend-exit directions. Shadow Learning may record and evaluate their incremental future-outcome value, but it starts with zero effect on screens and cannot automatically alter live weights, thresholds, alerts, or monitoring.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires quality controls to prevent a noisy short-lived opportunity score from becoming a high-grade signal, while retaining real-time evidence integrity and Shadow Learning isolation.

**How to apply:** Keep opportunity scoring, confidence, cross-context evidence, and counter-evidence as distinct explainable outputs. Treat unavailable data as unavailable, preserve fail-closed stage promotion, and require audited real future samples plus human approval before any weight adjustment.
