# Conversation Capture Gate
Not every chat line becomes memory.

Flow: Conversation → Candidate → Classification → Evidence check → Admission or reject.
Classes: R Reality Evidence Candidate | P Owner Preference | D Owner Decision | I Interface Feedback | H Hypothesis | U Unknown | O Outcome.

P ≠ Reality. D ≠ Evidence. AI interpretation ≠ ground truth.
Reuse preclassification inbox. No Promotion. Append-only candidates.
