# Alpha Knowledge Objects
Stable IDs. Append-only. Outcome never rewrites a prior object.
productionAuthority stays false. Not live scoring.

Types:
- AR — Research Record: what anomaly/question was seen; origin; time; unknowns.
- AI — Intelligence Record: state after evidence + causal reasoning; links to AR ids.
- AD — Decision Record: who decided what under which intelligence ids; not evidence.
- AO — Outcome Record: what reality did later; industry / fundamental / economic / security / execution.
- AA — Attribution Record: causal root of AO; not price-up=correct.

ID format: {TYPE}-{YYYYMMDD}-{slug}
